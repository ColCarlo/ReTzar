Uther Pendragon, humiliated, found in this defiance the perfect excuse to destroy the duke and seize his lands, his vassals and... his wife. Thanks to his magic arts the king managed to deceive Igraine: three hours after her husband had died in battle, Uther Pendragon, in the likeness of the duke, lay with his beloved.\n\nWhen Igraine learned that she was a widow, she had no choice but to submit to the will of the king, who in the end took her as his wife.\n\nNine months after that deceit a child was born, and the time came for the king to pay for his lust, handing over to Merlin the fruit of his passion for Igraine.
