# Game data

ReTzar's code is free software under the GPL-3.0-or-later (see LICENSE).
The game data are not. That means everything of Tzar that ReTzar ships in
`ReTzar.dat`: graphics, sounds, music, voices, movies, maps, campaigns and
texts. It also covers the original texts quoted in `languages` and the
icon.

They belong to their owners:

- Haemimont Games, who made Tzar
- Take-Two Interactive, who hold the rights of TalonSoft's editions
- FX Interactive, for its editions, campaigns and dubbing

They are included with their kind permission, as long as they stay free:
they may be shared at no charge, never sold. They are not open source and
the GPL does not cover them.

ReTzar starts only beside a copy of Tzar of any edition.
